Support

We usually reply within two days.

The quickest way to reach us is from inside the app: open You, then Send feedback. You can attach a screenshot, and it tells us which version you're using. Or email hello@getmunib.com (hello@getmunib.com).

Common questions

How do I cancel Munib Plus?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Munib, and choose Cancel Subscription. If you cancel during the 30-day free trial, you won't be charged.

I bought Munib Plus but it isn't showing

Open You in the app and tap Restore purchases, making sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I delete my account?

Open You, scroll to Account, and tap Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, progress, saved ayat, likes and reflections. If you subscribe, cancel in Settings as well.

The recitation isn't playing

Tap the card once: a single tap pauses and plays. Check your phone isn't on silent with the volume down, and that you're connected to the internet (recitation streams; the text works offline for surahs you've already opened).

How do I change the translation or reciter?

Open You and choose from 7 translations and 4 reciters.

Someone posted something upsetting

Tap Report next to the reflection, and Block if you don't want to see that person again. We review reports within 24 hours.

I found a mistake in a translation or note

Thank you. Please send it to us with Send feedback or by email, with the ayah number, and we'll check it carefully.

If you're struggling

Munib isn't a substitute for real support. In the UK you can call Samaritans free on 116 123 at any time, or the Muslim Youth Helpline on 0808 808 2008. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 999.
