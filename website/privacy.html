Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

The short version. We collect only what Munib needs to work. We never sell or share your data, we show no ads, and we use no advertising or tracking tools. You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Munib is made by Sajawal Naeem, trading as Munib Labs, in London, United Kingdom. We are the "controller" of your personal data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). You can contact us at hello@getmunib.com (hello@getmunib.com).

What we collect, and why

Your mood choices, translation, reciter and reminder times stay on your phone. Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone; no reminder data is sent to us.

Religious information

Using a Quran app can reveal your religion, which UK law treats as "special category" data that needs extra care. We process it only to provide Munib to you, on the basis of your explicit consent when you create an account. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of service providers, under contracts that only let them process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and accounts, in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple or subscribe to Munib Plus. Apple handles all payments; we never see your card details.
  • The Quran Foundation (Quran.com), which provides the Quran text, translations, recitation and tafsir. Our server requests this content for you; we don't send them any information about you. Audio files are streamed from their servers, which see your device's internet address, as any website would.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone else, and we will never use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account in the app, your profile, likes, saved ayat, progress and reflections are deleted immediately. Feedback is kept for up to 2 years so we can follow up on problems. You can ask us to remove yourself from the early access list at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy of it, object to or limit how we use it, and withdraw consent. Email hello@getmunib.com and we'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

You must be 13 or over to create a Munib account. Anyone can read the Quran tab without an account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and our database uses row-level security, so each person can only access their own private data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
