## What Munib is

**Mission (Saj, 2 Nov 2026): onboarding the world to Islam.** Munib is the easiest first step into the Quran, for anyone. The first audience is still Muslims whose attention has been shaped by social media, but decisions should also work for someone meeting the Quran for the first time (shared ayah pages, plain-English context, nothing that assumes prior knowledge).

**Saj's framing (13 Nov 2026):** the goal is to help people return to Allah; replace the doomscrolling habit with something useful; be the central hub for Muslims; long term, unite the ummah. He wants to build out many features (Muslim Pro is the main competitor). Advice given: the feed stays the front door (the doomscroll replacement), utilities are table stakes so people can leave Muslim Pro, lead marketing with what Muslim Pro can't do, trust shown not argued (no tracking), and as features grow, organise them into clear sections (a tab bar) rather than burying them under You.

**Long-term vision (Saj, 2 Nov 2026):** a single destination to begin and grow your journey in Islam. The Quran is the starting point; later, an educational hub with video lessons by category (learning about Islam, fitness, finances, relationships), all through an Islamic lens. Not being built yet: launch and prove the Quran app first. Ideas Saj floated: everything inside the one app, perhaps £9.99/month for the hub; AI-produced short and long videos rather than human lecturers. Saj (9 Nov 2026) is also weighing a free, ad-supported social version (vetted Shariah-compliant advertisers, ads not dominating the feed; goal: unite Muslims and onboard new Muslims, with the website helping new Muslims). Saj's shape for it (9 Nov 2026): no exploitative or mood-based data collection; ads only on For you, as their own separate card, never interrupting an ayah; user posts: see below; keep the 20-minute break card; a paid plan removes ads. Not built yet (launch first); advice given: if ever, contextual direct-sold sponsors only, no ad networks or profiling (mood and religion are special-category data), never on the Quran tab, during recitation or on grief/divorce moods, check QF terms, and beware engagement incentives undermining "success = coming back, not time-on-app". **Muslim business deals (Saj, 14 Nov 2026, idea, not built):** a section linking to Muslim businesses with discount codes, "like a coupon website but within the app". A short-form, TikTok-style Quran app for Muslims whose attention has been shaped by social media and who struggle to read the Quran. It scrolls like a social feed, but every card is an ayah, and it **gently stretches attention**: single ayat → passages → whole surahs.

**Success = people coming back and reading more, not time-on-app.** Design choices follow from that (time-check card after 20 minutes, no streak guilt, one notification a day maximum).

## Releasing an update (once on the App Store)

1. Make the change, type-check, push.
2. Small JavaScript-only fixes: `npx eas-cli@latest update` sends them straight to users' phones.
3. Anything with new native modules or app.json changes: `npx eas-cli@latest build -p ios`, test on TestFlight with 2–3 people for a day, then submit for review.
