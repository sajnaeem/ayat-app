## What Munib is

**Mission (Saj, 2 Nov 2026): onboarding the world to Islam.** Munib is the easiest first step into the Quran, for anyone. The first audience is still Muslims whose attention has been shaped by social media, but decisions should also work for someone meeting the Quran for the first time (shared ayah pages, plain-English context, nothing that assumes prior knowledge).

**Long-term vision (Saj, 2 Nov 2026):** a single destination to begin and grow your journey in Islam. The Quran is the starting point; later, an educational hub with video lessons by category (learning about Islam, fitness, finances, relationships), all through an Islamic lens. Not being built yet: launch and prove the Quran app first. Ideas Saj floated: everything inside the one app, perhaps £9.99/month for the hub; AI-produced short and long videos rather than human lecturers. Saj (9 Nov 2026) is also weighing a free, ad-supported social version (vetted Shariah-compliant advertisers, ads not dominating the feed; goal: unite Muslims and onboard new Muslims, with the website helping new Muslims). Saj's shape for it (9 Nov 2026): no exploitative or mood-based data collection; ads only on For you, as their own separate card, never interrupting an ayah; user posts: see below; keep the 20-minute break card; a paid plan removes ads. Not built yet (launch first); advice given: if ever, contextual direct-sold sponsors only, no ad networks or profiling (mood and religion are special-category data), never on the Quran tab, during recitation or on grief/divorce moods, check QF terms, and beware engagement incentives undermining "success = coming back, not time-on-app". **User content (Saj, 9 Nov 2026, reversing "no user posts" from earlier the same day):** let Muslim creators (invited from other platforms) and eventually anyone with an account post Islamic content, with a review process before anything is published, because Munib alone can't make enough content. **Built 9 Nov 2026:** `supabase/006_posts.sql` (applied): `posts` (text / image / video up to 90s and 50 MB, caption ≤600, optional source, up to 3 moods; status pending → approved / rejected; RLS: others only see approved, unhidden, unblocked posts; authors see their own; max 10 pending each; guidelines must be agreed), `profiles.is_reviewer` (set only in the dashboard; app column grants stop self-promotion), `review_post()` RPC, `post_reports` (3 reports hide a post and send it back to pending), private `posts` storage bucket read through signed links, likes/comments reuse `likes`/`reflections` with key `p:<id>` (limit raised to 48). App: `src/lib/posts.ts`, `src/app/post.tsx` (create; from You → "Create a post"), `src/app/review.tsx` (reviewer queue with approve / turn down + reason shown to the author), `src/components/PostCard.tsx`, You screen lists your posts with status; For you shows approved posts in every other extras slot (mood-matched first, each once per visit) with like, comment (reflections sheet) and report/block. To make someone a reviewer: Table Editor → profiles → is_reviewer = true. **AI first pass (10 Nov 2026):** `supabase/007_ai_review.sql` (applied) adds `ai_verdict` (ok / check / reject), `ai_summary`, `ai_notes`, `ai_checked_at` to posts (authors can't set them: insert policy). Edge function `moderate` (`supabase/functions/moderate/index.ts`, verify_jwt on; secret `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, model `claude-haiku-5-5`, override with `MODERATION_MODEL`) is called by the app right after a post is created (`aiCheck` in posts.ts; reviewers can re-run it from the review screen). It checks text, source and photos against the guidelines, compares any `s:a` Quran references with the real text, and saves a verdict; `reject` turns the post down with a kind reason for the author, everything else still needs a person to approve. Videos: caption only (notes say to watch it); next steps are transcription + frames and CSAM hash matching (e.g. Cloudflare's tool once media moves to Cloudflare). Review queue is sorted check → unchecked → ok. Privacy policy names Anthropic as a processor. **Ask about this ayah (10 Nov 2026, Saj's choice over pre-written explanations):** an "Ask" button on every card (VerseCard, next to Keep reading) opens `AskSheet` (src/components/Study.tsx): three starter questions plus free text (3–400 chars); signed-in only (guests see a sign-up prompt, signin reason `ask`). Edge function `ask` (`supabase/functions/ask/index.ts`, verify_jwt on, model `claude-sonnet-5-5` via `ASK_MODEL`) answers from Tafsir Ibn Kathir for the ayah (via `qf`) and the ayat around it (−4/+3) only: under 180 words, plain text, no rulings (points to a scholar or local imam), never invents hadith or references, crisis → Samaritans. Personal questions and answers are not stored; `ask_usage` (008_ask.sql, applied, service-only) counts typed questions per person per day (limit 5, `ASK_PER_DAY`). The three starter questions (`ASK_STARTERS` in src/lib/ask.ts, must match `STARTERS` in the function) are answered once per ayah and kept in `ask_cache` (key `s:a#n`, service-only; delete a row to have it rewritten), served free and outside the limit. Cost decision (Saj, 10 Nov 2026): keep Munib free; cut Ask costs with the cache and the lower limit; add an optional "Support Munib" contribution (in-app purchase tip, Apple takes 15%) before Ramadan; "Munib Plus" (unlimited Ask and extras) only as a fallback if costs outrun support. Readers can report an answer (goes to the feedback table with the question and answer). Same `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` secret as moderation; without it the sheet says Ask isn't switched on yet. Answers need scholar spot-checks before launch. Advice given: pre-approve every post (nothing goes live unreviewed), creators first then open submissions, scholar check for religious claims, the UK Online Safety Act applies to user-to-user services (risk assessment, illegal-content duties, reporting), and moderation cost grows with volume. **Decided (2 Nov 2026): AI voiceover over b-roll footage, no fake on-screen people, and a real person approves every script** (also advised: AI for production, but scripts written or checked by named scholars, every Quran and hadith quote verified, no AI rulings, no AI-generated faces presented as real teachers, and AI voices disclosed).

A short-form, TikTok-style Quran app for Muslims whose attention has been shaped by social media and who struggle to read the Quran. It scrolls like a social feed, but every card is an ayah, and it **gently stretches attention**: single ayat → passages → whole surahs.

**Success = people coming back and reading more, not time-on-app.** Design choices follow from that (time-check card after 20 minutes, no streak guilt, one notification a day maximum).

## Product decisions (don't reverse without asking Saj)

- **Name:** Munib (منيب, "one who turns back to Allah", 50:33). Bundle ID `app.munib`, scheme `munib://`. Domain getmunib.com; company/developer name Munib Labs. ("Ayat" and "Awwab" were dropped; Awwab clashed with an existing Islamic app.)
- **Pricing: free, no subscription (Saj, 10 Nov 2026; replaces the 31 Oct plan of a 30-day trial then £0.99/month or £9/year, and the earlier £3.99 plan).** The whole app is free. Monetisation later through advertising on For you (see the ad-supported version under Long-term vision for the rules), so no paywall or RevenueCat work. Advice given: ads only pay at scale (100k+ monthly users), so costs need runway until then; optional voluntary support (e.g. sadaqah-style "support Munib") could cover costs without ads. Since 31 Oct 2026 the **Quran tab works without an account** (guests land there; For you, reflections and You open `src/app/signin.tsx` with a reason).
- **Content supply (Saj, 10 Nov 2026):** For you becomes a social feed: Munib's own content, reviewed user posts and the Quran. Saj plans to hire clip farmers to cut short clips from publicly available scholar and imam podcasts and post them (in Munib and on social media as top-of-funnel). Advice given: publicly available is not permission; get written permission from each channel or speaker, credit and link the source on every clip, check clips don't change the meaning, and route them through the same review queue.
- **Never sell or share user data.** Religion is special-category data under UK GDPR. No ad networks.
- **iOS first.** Android later from the same code.
- **Accounts:** not required to read (Saj, 10 Nov 2026: guests now get the "What's on your heart?" picker on first open and can use For you, so the first two minutes show what Munib is before any sign-up; a gentle "Make Munib yours" `join` card appears after 8 For-you ayat and every 24 after, never a wall). Still required for reflections, Ask, posts, You and progress backup. Sign in with Apple and email for the iOS launch; Google and phone (SMS, paid per text) are coded but hidden (`SHOW_GOOGLE` / `SHOW_PHONE` in SignIn.tsx).
- **Design: "Quiet Modern".** Light, soft tinted grounds rotating through 5 tones per card; large right-set Arabic (Noto Naskh Arabic); Literata for reading; Bricolage Grotesque for UI. Avoid the Amiri look that competitors use. Subtle entrance animation: Arabic appears word by word, right to left, and arrives alone; the line, translation and buttons follow once it's complete. Controls (top bar, side buttons, card buttons) always stay visible (an auto-fade was tried and removed on 31 Oct 2026 at Saj's request). A faint eight-point star motif sits in two corners of each card. Icon: cream منيب on emerald (#1F6E52) with the same star; launch screen light #F1F3EF / dark #121A16.
- **Descriptive labels (Saj approved 2 Nov 2026, after Rory Sutherland's *Alchemy*):** honest context makes the same thing feel more valuable, so tell the real story around the ayat, never embellish the ayat themselves. Done: "Revealed in Makkah/Madinah" on surah-opening cards and share images, "Makkan/Madinan" in the surah list (`SURAHS[i][4]`, standard Mushaf classification from quran-json; a few surahs' classification is debated by scholars), reciters with place and story (`RECITERS` notes in audio.ts), tafsir credited as "the classic 14th-century commentary by Ibn Kathir of Damascus", mood sub-lines written as moments ("For a racing mind and a heavy chest"). Still to do: paywall copy in the same style ("Every reciter, from Makkah to Cairo. The classic tafsir of Ibn Kathir."), App Store listing, reel hooks such as "Recited by the Imam of the Grand Mosque in Makkah". Any historical claim (e.g. a surah's occasion of revelation, al-Husary being among the first to record the whole Quran) goes on the scholar's review list.
- **Infinite feed**, not a fixed daily set. Two tabs at the top left (TikTok-style): **For you** (curated ayat by mood; random whole-Quran ayat are deliberately not mixed in, as many need their context) and **Quran** (the whole Quran in order as bite-size cards, `orderCards` in quran.ts groups very short ayat up to 4 / ~10 words; a surah-opening card with the basmalah; resumes from `prefs.place`, saved as each card is read). Mood pill shows only on For you; the book button on both. No speaker/mute button: tapping a card pauses (sticky across cards); users who had muted in older versions start paused. Curated cards switch to the Quran Foundation text once their surah loads (`liveVerse`, prefetched for the next cards), bundled text is the fallback. Loaded surahs are saved on the phone for offline reading (7 days max per QF terms, newest 16).

## Releasing an update (once on the App Store)

1. Make the change, type-check, push.
2. Small JavaScript-only fixes: `npx eas-cli@latest update` sends them straight to users' phones.
3. Anything with new native modules or app.json changes: `npx eas-cli@latest build -p ios`, test on TestFlight with 2–3 people for a day, then submit for review.
